# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, professional, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- discuss technical and scientific disagreements on their merits;
- distinguish evidence, assumptions, and preferences;
- give actionable review feedback;
- respect research integrity, authorship, privacy, and provenance concerns;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and deliberate disruption.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information without permission, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact the repository owner through the private contact mechanisms available on their GitHub profile.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

This document is intentionally concise. As the contributor community grows, the project may adopt a standard community code such as Contributor Covenant with a dedicated enforcement contact.
